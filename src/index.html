About DiabetesCare

DiabetesCare is a free, open-source diabetes management app built by Tebogo Maruping as a portfolio project demonstrating React/HTML5, data visualisation, and health UX design.

This app is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from your healthcare team. Always consult your doctor or diabetes specialist for medical decisions.
